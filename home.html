Welcome to the Online Book Store

This web site is the online book store of GL Bajaj Institute of Technology & Management. It keeps the text books and reference books prescribed for every branch of engineering in one place, so that a student can find a title, check its author, publisher and price, and order it without leaving the campus portal.

Pick a branch — CSE, ECE, EEE or CIVIL — from the left frame and the catalogue for that branch opens here in the right frame. Use the links in the top frame to log in, register a new account, browse the complete catalogue or review your cart.

Branch wise Catalogue

Books arranged branch by branch with cover, author, publisher and price.

Simple Cart

Add a book to the cart, change the quantity and see the total instantly.

Member Accounts

Register once, log in, and place your order in a single click.

How to use this site

  1. Choose your branch from the left frame.
  2. Click Add to Cart against the books you need.
  3. Open the Cart page from the top frame and check the total.
  4. Login (or register first) and place the order.
